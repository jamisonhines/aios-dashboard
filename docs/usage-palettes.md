# Co-occurrence-safe usage palettes

Computed and validated: 2026-10-01. Executable hex values and fixed retained-model bindings: `usagePalettes.mjs`. The original adjacent-only palette is superseded.

## Computed, not hand-picked

`dev/search-usage-palettes.mjs` runs a bounded deterministic offline search. It samples provider hue grids and lightness/chroma grids, projects into sRGB, and rejects candidates outside the actual measured hue range, the mode's lightness band, or chroma floor. Hue ranges are Claude 310 through 360 and 0 through 100 (magenta/rose through yellow), OpenAI 130 through 285 (green through blue/violet), local 290 through 335 (violet/magenta). Gamut clipping is accounted for before hue-range checks.

The optimizer uses fixed farthest-point restarts and coordinate ascent to maximize the minimum normal-vision OKLab Delta E, subject to Machado 2009 severity-1 protan/deutan Delta E >=6. It searches providers independently and then jointly, including cross-provider pairs. Crowded joint candidates receive eight restarts and one ascent pass; at most twelve colours receive 96 restarts and three passes. Budgets depend on cardinality, never elapsed time or randomness. This is a bounded heuristic search, not a proof of global optimality or impossibility.

When a joint candidate fails, the next smallest whole-export token share within a provider is folded, with stable lexical tie breaks. The first passing joint capacity has four Claude, four OpenAI, and three local steps. The resulting hex values below are committed, not generated by the plugin. A one-member fold that saves no colour is restored to its individual identity.

Reproduce using a private export and temporary output, without committing usage data:

```sh
node dev/search-usage-palettes.mjs path/to/private-export.json /tmp/usage-palette-result.json
node dev/validate-usage-palettes.mjs path/to/private-export.json /tmp/aios-dataviz/validate_palette.js /tmp/usage-palette-evidence.md
```

## Whole-export grouping, no recolour-on-filter

`computeUsageColorPlan` receives the entire export once, before selecting a range. If a provider has more models than steps, it retains its largest models by input + cache read + cache write + output token share and reserves its last step for `<Provider> other`. Claude and OpenAI can retain three identities plus one Other; local can show all three identities, or two plus Other if it overflows. The chosen plan is passed to both chart modes, legend, table, and single-day chart. Paging or changing Cost/Tokens cannot re-rank that plan.

Retained known identities bind their explicit steps, never their visible rank. Retained unknown identities take the next unused provider step, and fallback assignments are saved in plugin settings. Folded models share only their named provider Other series as permitted by the fix-round ruling. The chart combines their four buckets and cost; the model table keeps every individual name, every bucket and total, and labels its Other membership. The UI also lists all current folds. Exported per-model data and prices are unchanged.

The implementation report records exactly which models folded on the real export and their whole-provider token shares. No account usage totals or transcripts are stored in this public document or fixtures.

## Validation gate

`usagePalettePairs.test.mjs` tests all reserved pairs in both modes, whole-export ranking, stable identities across a reversed-rank selected day, unknown fallback slots, and preservation of tokens and cost. The canonical external validator runs `--pairs all` on each provider, the combined palette, and every actual day's co-occurring displayed groups, in both modes. Folded identities are one labelled displayed series, not falsely claimed as individually colour-separated. The report includes every run and exit status. Passing adjacent order alone is never accepted.
